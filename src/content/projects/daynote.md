Daynote is a daily planning application designed to help you stay organised without unnecessary complexity.

## Overview

Daynote combines task tracking, routine planning, and simple scheduling into a clean, distraction-free experience. Rather than overwhelming dashboards and cluttered views, Daynote focuses on giving you a clear understanding of what needs to be done, when, and why.

## Key Principles

- **Designed for clarity** — A focused interface that shows you what matters without visual noise.
- **Privacy-first** — Your data stays on your device. Optional sync is available, but nothing is stored on developer-controlled servers.
- **Minimal friction** — Quick entry and simple organisation to reduce the gap between intention and action.

## Status

Daynote is under active development. Features and scope may change as the application evolves.
